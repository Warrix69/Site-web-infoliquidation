Data destruction

The protection of information is of the utmost importance to Info Liquidation Recycle.

Request a pick-up

We are aware that all kinds of secret and confidential data, related to a company's activities or to people's private lives, end up on the hard drives and storage media we recover. That is why we pay very special attention to it: we destroy all of this data using software with powerful algorithms that meet the standards of the RCMP, NASA and the U.S. Department of Defense.

To meet our clients' requirements, confidential data destruction certificates can be issued at their request, free of charge.

If the recovered equipment is unusable, the best and most effective solution is the complete destruction of all storage media, so that the data can never be used by anyone again.

Good to know!

Even if you think you have deleted all the information from your computer, some data cannot be erased from the hard drive without a formatting process that makes data recovery software useless. Authorities such as the Royal Canadian Mounted Police (RCMP) and the OPC recommend formatting each hard drive between three and seven times, at 45 minutes per formatting. If you plan to donate your computer to an organization or a recycling company like Info Liquidation Recycle, know that most have strict confidentiality clauses.

  • U.S. Department of Defense
