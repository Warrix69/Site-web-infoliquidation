Recovery procedure

100% of computer components, obsolete or not, can be reclaimed and recycled.

Request a pick-up
1

CRT screens

After dismantling the casings and electronic boards, everything is separated manually and sent to different centres to recycle the plastic and metals. The material is then melted down to be reinjected into industries producing all kinds of products.

2

LCD, LED or plasma screens

Screens are sorted by type, as their distinct components call for different treatment solutions. For standard LCD screens with backlighting, the main challenge is separating the fluorescent tubes that contain mercury, a toxic substance. These tubes follow a dedicated stream where shredders separate the glass from the mercury-containing powder for neutralization and disposal.

3

Computer components

Devices are recovered, dismantled, shredded and crushed, manually or using specialized machines. Recoverable fragments are extracted: ferrous metals, non-ferrous metals, cables, plastics and other materials. Each type of equipment has its specific end-of-life destination.
