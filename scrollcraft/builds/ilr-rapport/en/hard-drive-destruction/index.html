On-site hard drive destruction

Your hard drives are destroyed securely, in compliance with environmental ethical rules. Hard drives contain polluting heavy metals and must not be thrown out with ordinary garbage.

Request a pick-up

Why use a specialized organization?

Formatting a hard drive is not enough: slight residual magnetization can remain and allow the data to be read again with specialized devices.

  • Collection on agreed dates or one-time destruction
  • Certificate issued after every destruction service
  • Approved secure recycling

Where to dispose of old hard drives?

Proper disposal of your hard drives is crucial to prevent leaks of sensitive information and protect the environment. Our specialized services ensure the responsible and secure disposal of your old devices.

How is hard drive destruction carried out?

Shredding

The drives are reduced to small chips: platters, mechanisms and electronic components are completely destroyed, making the data unrecoverable.

Crushing

A pressure of at least 3,400 kg is applied to the magnetic platters, punching an irreparable hole through them: the data becomes inaccessible.

The 5 steps of the destruction process

1

Secure storage

The drives are placed in secure containers awaiting a regular or one-time collection.

2

Weighing, destruction and traceability

Our team weighs the drives before destruction, then provides a destruction certificate and a waste tracking slip.

3

Transport to a recycling centre

The residues are transported to a specialized centre and poured into a recycling pit.

4

Recycling

The material obtained from your hard drive chips becomes the base substance that will be reclaimed.

5

Second destruction

Non-recyclable elements undergo another destruction phase.

Frequently asked questions about hard drive destruction

Is it possible to recover data after erasing a hard drive with software?
Yes, in some cases data can be recovered after erasure. That is why physical destruction is recommended as a more reliable method.
Can damaged hard drives be destroyed safely?
Yes, damaged drives can be destroyed safely, provided you inform the service of the devices' condition.
How long does it take to destroy a hard drive?
The timeframe depends on the method used and the number of drives. The process is relatively quick, especially if you use a professional service.
Can I destroy my hard drives myself?
Personal destruction is possible with the appropriate equipment and knowledge. However, calling on experts is recommended for greater security.
What are the environmental standards for hard drive destruction?
Standards vary from country to country. Choose a service that complies with the regional regulations in force.
What happens to the debris resulting from hard drive destruction?
The debris resulting from hard drive destruction can be recycled where possible; some services offer suitable recycling options.
