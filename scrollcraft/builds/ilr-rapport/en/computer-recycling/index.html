Computer and laptop recycling

After a certain period of use, your electronic devices become obsolete or defective. In many cases, old devices pile up in a room and are forgotten. Yet you should dispose of all these items that clutter your space and pollute the environment.

Request a pick-up

Why recycle a laptop?

Laptop recycling is an important step in preserving the environment and avoiding data confidentiality risks. By entrusting your laptop's recycling to our qualified professionals, you help reduce electronic waste that is harmful to nature, lower your ecological footprint and secure your personal information.

What is WEEE?

All devices running on electric power are called waste electrical and electronic equipment (WEEE). When a device bears the crossed-out garbage bin logo, it means it must never be thrown in the trash.

  • Computers
  • Printers
  • Central processing units
  • Cathode-ray screens
  • Keyboards
  • Mice
  • Modems
  • Mobile / cell phones
  • Cables, chargers, batteries

The benefits of recycling your equipment

Recycling protects the environment, contributes to the economy and creates jobs, by giving a new life to your electronic equipment and devices.

Protecting the environment and natural resources

Preserving nature is one of the major outcomes of recycling: it reduces the amount of waste released into nature as well as the toxic and hazardous substances dispersed in it. Electronic components are made of natural resources, gold, copper, aluminum, that are non-renewable and finite.

Job creation

Another benefit of recycling electronic devices is job creation. This fast-growing sector recruits nearly 8,000 people every year.

Frequently asked questions about computer recycling

Why shouldn't I throw my laptop in the garbage?
Throwing a laptop in the garbage may seem like a quick fix, but it carries major environmental risks. Laptops contain toxic materials, lead, mercury, cadmium, that can contaminate soil and groundwater, affecting local wildlife and plants. Moreover, landfills are already overflowing with electronic waste. By recycling, you help reduce pollution and preserve our precious natural resources.
Where can I recycle my laptop?
Recycling your laptop is easier than you think. Many local recycling centres have collection points for electronic devices. You can also turn to certified recycling companies specialized in the responsible processing of end-of-life electronic equipment.
What if my laptop is still in good working order?
If it is still functional, you can extend its useful life: donate it to a charity that redistributes these devices to people in need, or sell it at an affordable price. This approach promotes reuse and minimizes electronic waste.
How can I securely erase my data before recycling my laptop?
Use reputable data erasure software: it securely overwrites all the information stored on your hard drive, making it virtually impossible to recover. Choose reliable software, preferably recommended by IT security experts.
Is laptop recycling free?
In many cases, yes, especially at local recycling centres or during special collection events. Some centres may however charge processing fees: check their pricing policy. At Info Liquidation Recycle, pick-up and processing are entirely free.
What happens once my laptop is recycled?
Reusable components, RAM, hard drives, screen, are extracted and prepared for a new use. Non-recyclable materials are processed responsibly, often transformed into building materials or solid fuels. Metals and plastics are recovered to manufacture new electronic products: a more circular and sustainable economy.
