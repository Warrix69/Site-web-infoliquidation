Privacy · Law 25

Cookie policy

This policy explains how Info Liquidation Recycle uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit infoliquidationrecycle.com, in compliance with Quebec's Law 25 and applicable Canadian laws.

Request a pick-up

What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small text file placed on your device by the site you visit. It allows the site to recognize your browser, remember certain choices (such as language) and measure page audience.

Cookies we use

We only use strictly necessary cookies for the site to function (language preferences, form submissions, security). No analytics or advertising cookies are activated without your prior consent.

Your consent

On your first visit, a banner informs you about cookie use. You can accept or refuse non-essential cookies. You can change your choice at any time by clearing cookies from your browser.

Retention period

Strictly necessary cookies are kept for the duration of the session or for a maximum of 12 months. No personal data is associated with these cookies.

Your rights

Under Law 25, you may at any time request access, correction or deletion of personal information we hold about you. For questions, email info@infoliquidationrecycle.com.

Changes

We may update this cookie policy. The last update date appears at the bottom of the page. Changes take effect as soon as they are published on the site.
